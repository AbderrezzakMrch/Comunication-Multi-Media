Presentation Bullet Points
==========================

Overview
- Flask-based adaptive streaming demo for rapid experimentation.
- Upload once, receive multi-resolution renditions plus segmented outputs.
- Lightweight client showcases manual switching, auto adaptation, and simulated bandwidth.

Architecture
- `main.py` orchestrates Flask app creation, configuration, and blueprint registration.
- `streaming_app.routes` hosts upload/list/delete/media endpoints and session wiring.
- `streaming_app.services` centralizes ffmpeg/ffprobe helpers, manifest handling, and validation.
- Single-page UI (`templates/index.html`) styled via `static/css/style.css`, powered by `static/js/main.js`.

Processing Workflow
- Secure upload validation and slugified playlist IDs per session.
- Probes source metadata, filters requested resolutions to avoid upscaling.
- Transcodes with synced keyframes, segments to aligned clips, computes bitrates.
- Writes `manifest.json` plus organized `variants/` and `segments/` folders under `media/`.

Player Experience
- Auto loads most recent playlist, shows playlist history sidebar.
- Video card supports full video playback, per-segment jumps, and resolution chips.
- Auto adapt monitors throughput (or simulator input) to upgrade/downgrade intelligently.
- Bandwidth simulator and delete protections highlight testing and lifecycle UX.

Why It Matters
- Demonstrates end-to-end adaptive streaming pipeline without external services.
- FFmpeg-driven processing is transparent and easily configurable.
- Clear separation of concerns simplifies onboarding and future feature work.

Next Steps (Optional Talking Points)
- Add authentication/roles, task queue, or cloud storage integration.
- Export to HLS/DASH manifests for production-grade streaming.
- Extend front-end analytics (segment metrics, bandwidth charts).
